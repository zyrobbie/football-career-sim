## Long careers

48 unique seeds, `FUNV3-LONG-00` through `FUNV3-LONG-47`, cycle ST/LW/RW/CAM/LM/RM/CM/CDM/LB/RB/CB. Every seed is created through public identity, academy, event, contract and training actions with legacy OFF rules until the first adult (age 18, W10) editable plan. That exact raw fixture is cloned for all four groups, changing only the V3 root. The common business projection SHA must agree before any group action.

- OFF: root created with `createEntertainmentV3State(selectedClubId, 56)`, LEGACY_CAP, no purchase. Exact business-state projections after every public action are checked separately against original V2.5 code, not against another candidate path.
- ON_FREE: activation 0, UNCAPPED, no purchase.
- ON_FIXED_BODY: activation 0, UNCAPPED. At each eligible yearly plan, BODY, highest affordable tier PREMIUM -> PLUS -> BASIC, no reserve. Evaluate once per game year at the first adult, valid-contract editable plan. Saturated/ineligible/insufficient cash means skip that year, without a winter retry.
- ON_SITUATIONAL: activation 0, UNCAPPED. At each eligible editable plan while that year's quota remains unused, choose BODY when fitness <55, else MENTAL when morale <55, else SETTLE with a valid move and morale or squad relation <65, else skip. Highest affordable tier after retaining at least one half-year's disposable salary; cannot fall back to another category to evade the chosen category's eligibility. Non-purchase does not consume an in-game quota.

Market policy cycles STAY_FIRST / PLATFORM_FIRST / PLAYING_TIME_FIRST by seed index modulo 3, with the established fullCareerAudit ordering. Academy offer index is seed index modulo offer count; coach arrival; no transfer-arrival extra. Training cycles BALANCED+STEADY, position-focused+STEADY, physical+TEAM_FIRST by seed index modulo 3. Before W2 it is BALANCED and no approach. Event setup/choice is the (seed index + window index + step index) modulo eligible ordered choices. This does not depend on action count or purchases. Key moments use the first offered choice through both public actions. No counteroffer/preference editing, no manual retirement before mandatory age/end-contract retirement. Stop only at CAREER_RETIRED or a reproducible error. No business result is locked to equalize ON groups.

Repeat accounting: adjacent windows with exactly the same event ID; adjacent ordinary event categories; story completion/expiry/transfer/retirement closures; unique reached V3 node IDs. Observations distinguish initial-plan low-state windows and explicit report recovery markers, not an inferred claim that all low state remained low after events.

## Cash A/B

20 unique seeds, `FUNV3-CASH-00` through `FUNV3-CASH-19`; five per fixed annual salary group: 18,000 / 60,000 / 240,000 / 2,000,000 euro. Public legacy prelude to W10, then an explicitly constructed controlled salary fixture: contract remainingHalfYears=16 and salary as grouped, initial cash=1.5 annual disposable income. Eight completed public-store windows W10..17, STAY_FIRST, other policy decisions use the same deterministic rules. The contract has enough duration not to trigger a new salary during the measured interval. This is a controlled experiment, not a natural salary sample.

Four arms per start: LEGACY_CAP and UNCAPPED with fixed highest-affordable BODY policy, plus no-spend LEGACY_CAP and UNCAPPED. All arms activate V3 for comparable event handling; cashPolicy is the only difference inside each spending-policy pair. Divergent tier affordability is an effect to report, not corrected away. Per report: salary due, salary actual (report stipend), cap loss, arrangement payment/refund, balance, and next report salary / prior payment. The ratio is replenishment, not incremental profit. Other event cash effects are recorded separately.

## Investigation and limitations

A category share >70% or absolute aggregate paired change >10% is an investigation flag, not a mechanical pass/fail. Zero-baseline cases are reported with absolute differences and no fabricated percent. Natural missing category/tier exposure cannot stand in for the separate nine-tier boundary matrix. Tests do not establish subjective fun, a human acceptance decision, or all-domain economic balance. Large raw trajectories are outputs; default CI regenerates fixtures and does not require machine-local captures.
