## Strong BODY effect, explicitly investigated

Fixed BODY versus free has average fitness+23.87%, minutes+8.65%, goals+10.88%, assists+13.17%, peakOVR+0.273%. Recovery338→5 is a98.5%relative reduction but15.38%→0.23%of total simulated windows. All48 fixed-BODY pairs produce more minutes;43 produce more goals and5 tie. This is a real mechanical preference signal for sustained BODY maintenance under this policy, not evidence of player enjoyment or proof that all category/tier tradeoffs are equally attractive.
